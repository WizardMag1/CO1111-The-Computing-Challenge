UCLan Cyprus • CO1111 The Computing Challenge

The Ultimate Campus Treasure Hunt Adventure

An interactive, mobile-first Web App connected to the CodeCyprus API. Solve location-based riddles, scan QR clues, track your GPS coordinates, and compete for the top score!

🎮 Play Now Learn More ↓

How It Works

Four simple steps to complete the treasure hunt and top the leaderboard.

1

Get a Clue

Request the next riddle from the server. Questions cannot be cached, ensuring fair play.

2

Go to Location

Navigate to the physical landmark. The app tracks your GPS and pings coordinates every 2 minutes.

3

Scan or Solve

Answer via tailored inputs (MCQ, Boolean, Numeric, Text) or scan physical on-site QR codes.

4

Earn Points & Win

Correct answers gain points, wrong answers cost points. Check live rankings on the leaderboard!

Engineered for Performance

Meeting every criteria of the CO1111 specification with commercial quality standards.

⚡ Adaptive Input Engine

Dynamically shifts input controls based on question type: 4-button MCQ grids, True/False toggles, numeric keypads, or text fields.

📍 Real-time Geolocation

Continuous GPS verification, periodic 2-minute server location synchronization, and interactive Leaflet map path tracking.

📷 Integrated QR Scanner

Camera-based QR reader decodes physical clue codes directly into the answer box or navigates to external clue URLs.

💾 Resilient Session Resume

State persistence in localStorage ensures players can refresh or navigate without losing their active session or score.

🏆 Live Leaderboard & Prizes

Real-time standings with instant score feedback, penalty warnings on question skips, and top prize indicators.

📱 Progressive Web App (PWA)

Installable on mobile home screens with standalone window display, Service Worker caching, and fast offline shell loading.
